## Overview

The Xinka, also called Xinca or Xinka, are a non-Mayan Indigenous people of Mesoamerica. Communities live in the southern portion of Guatemala near its border with El Salvador and in the mountainous region to the north. In the 2018 National Census, 264,167 individuals identified themselves as Xinka, representing 1.8% of Guatemala’s national population. The Xincan languages are not known to be related to any other language family, although they contain many loan words from Mayan languages. The Xinka are significant in the history of southeastern Guatemala, where they were present before the arrival of the Spanish and participated in political and cultural movements seeking recognition of their identity and rights.
