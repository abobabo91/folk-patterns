## Overview

The Huancas, also called Wancas or Wankas, are a Quechua people living in the Junín Region of central Peru, in and around the Mantaro Valley. Wanka Quechua is spoken in the southern part of Junín by the Wanka people and belongs to the Quechua I language group. The language has about 300,000 speakers and three main dialects: Waylla Wanka in Huancayo and Chupaca provinces, Waycha Wanka in Concepción, and Shawsha Wanka in Jauja. Historically, the Huanca cultivated root crops and maize and herded llama. Their recorded history includes incorporation into Tawantinsuyu, the Inca Empire, and an alliance with the Spaniards during the conquest of Peru.

## Foodways

## Language & religion

The Huanca speak Jauja Wanka Quechua and Waylla Wanka Quechua, both Quechua I languages. Wanka Quechua is also called Wanka Limay and Wanka Nunashimi. Its three main dialects are Waylla Wanka in Huancayo and Chupaca provinces, Waycha Wanka in Concepción, and Shawsha Wanka in Jauja. Wanka Quechua differs significantly from the Incas’ Quechua of Cusco and is similar to Ancash Quechua.
