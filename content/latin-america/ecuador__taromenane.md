## Overview

The Taromenane are an uncontacted Indigenous people living in Yasuni National Park in the Ecuadorian Amazon Basin. Together with the Tagaeri, they constitute the two last known Indigenous groups living in voluntary isolation in Ecuador. The Taromenane are believed to be distantly related to the Waorani people. An estimated 150–300 Taromenane continue to maintain a nomadic lifestyle in the rainforest, although this number is uncertain.

## Festivals & rituals

The available material does report that the Taromenane have been threatened by oil developments and illegal logging in Yasuni National Park. In February 2008, Ecuadorian authorities agreed to investigate reports that five tribespeople belonging to the Taromenane and Tagaeri tribes had been killed by illegal loggers.
