## Overview

The Kamëntšá are an Indigenous people in Colombia who primarily live in the Sibundoy Valley of the Putumayo Department in the south of Colombia. Their name is also rendered as Camsá, Camëntsëá, Coche, Kamemtxa, Kamsa, Kamse, Sibundoy, and Sibundoy-Gaché. The Kamëntšá language is a language isolate, written in the Latin script. Kamëntšá culture is documented here through carved wooden masks, cultivated food and entheogenic plants, ceremonial practices, shamans, and mythic narratives.

## Material culture

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

**Carved wooden masks** are worn during ceremonies and festivals. No ceramics, metalwork, tools, or household goods are documented.

## Dance & theatre

They mention carved wooden masks worn during ceremonies and festivals without describing the associated performances.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources state that carved wooden masks are worn during ceremonies and festivals. They also state that Kamëntšá people use ayahuasca, also called yagé, *Brugmansia* species, *Iochroma fuchsioides*, and *Desfontainia* in their rituals. Kamëntšá shamans are noted for the number and variety of *Brugmansia* cultivars they have propagated in gardens of entheogenic plants.

## Foodways

The Kamëntšá farm maize, beans, potatoes, and peas.

## Oral tradition & literature

John Holmes McDowell’s *“So Wise Were Our Elders”: Mythic Narratives of the Kamsá* contains mythic and legendary material in Camsá with interlinear morphemic glossing and English translations. Hugo Jamioy Juagibioy is identified as a poet and Indigenous rights activist.

## Language & religion

Kamëntšá is a language isolate and the native language of the Kamëntšá people. Researchers have tried connecting it to the Chibchan languages without success. The language is written in the Latin script and is described as polysynthetic, with prefixes and suffixes and a dual number. The sources list the names Coche, Mocoa, Sebondoy, Quillacinga, and Patoco in connection with varieties. They document the ritual use of ayahuasca or yagé, *Brugmansia* species, *Iochroma fuchsioides*, and *Desfontainia*, as well as the role of Kamëntšá shamans.
