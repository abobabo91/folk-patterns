## Overview

The Siriano, also called *Selea* or *Sürá*, are a Tucanoan Indigenous people of Colombia and Brazil. Their total population is estimated at 750, with most living in Colombia. Their culture is exogamous, and speakers are identified glossologically by the first language of their father. The available sources identify the Siriano language as Tucanoan and state that it is spoken in Colombia, with a few speakers in Brazil.
