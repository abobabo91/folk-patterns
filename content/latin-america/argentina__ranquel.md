## Overview

The Ranquel, also called Rankülche, are an Indigenous tribe from the northern part of La Pampa Province, Argentina, in South America. Their own name is explained through Mapudungun as *rankül* (“cane”) and *che* (“man, people”), or “cane-people.” In the late 18th and early 19th centuries, they controlled two chiefdoms. They were hunters and nomads, and during much of the 19th century maintained an alliance with the Tehuelche people. Their movements extended across the Cuarto and Colorado rivers, the south of San Luis and Córdoba, La Pampa, the western part of Buenos Aires Province, Mendoza, and Santa Fe. The sources state that they were conquered and assimilated into the Mapuche.

## Language & religion

The name Rankülche is explained through Mapudungun, with *rankül* meaning “cane” and *che* meaning “man, people.”
