## Overview

The Sart Kalmyks, also called Sart Kalmaks, are an ethnic group of the Oirats living in the villages of Chelpek and Börü-Bash in Issyk Kul Province, Kyrgyzstan. Their population is estimated at c. 12,000, although the available census figures differ: 5,824 Kalmyks were registered in the 1999 Kyrgyzstan census, while the 2009 Census recorded 3,800 Kalmyks in Kyrgyzstan. They are descendants of the Ööled tribes, and some part of their population inhabited the area during the rule of the Zunghar Khanate. They formerly spoke Sart Kalmyk, a dialect of the Oirat language, but have largely shifted to Kyrgyz. Their long co-inhabitance with Kyrgyz people has led to substantial incorporation into the Kyrgyz nation, although some Kyrgyz still regard them as distinct. Most Sart Kalmyks now identify as Kyrgyz or “almost Kyrgyz.” They belong to the Muslim faith.
