## Overview

The Aimaq Hazara, also known as *Hazara-ye Qala-ye Naw*, are the Hazara component of the Aimaq confederation in Afghanistan. The available source identifies them as mostly Sunni Muslims, distinguishing them from other Hazaras, who are mostly Shia Muslims. Some Aimaq Hazaras are semi-nomadic and live in yurts covered with felt. Their relevance to folk culture in the available material lies chiefly in their position within the Aimaq confederation, their religious distinction from other Hazaras, and the documented use of felt-covered yurts by some semi-nomadic members.

The Aimaq Hazara are mostly Sunni Muslims, while other Hazaras are mostly Shia Muslims. The source gives the name *Aimaq Hazara* in Dari as ایماق هزاره and supplies the romanized form *Aimāq Həzārə*.
