## Overview

The Madjars, also called the Madi-yar people, are a Turkic ethnic group in Kazakhstan. They number about 1,000–2,000 and live mostly in the Kostanay Region. The source describes them as predominantly Muslim. Their ethnonym has been interpreted by Turkologist Dr. Imre Baski as meaning “faithful Muslim,” literally “friend or follower of Muhammad,” and as deriving ultimately from Muhammad-i-yar. The group has also been linked onomastically to the Magyars, or Hungarians, but Imre Baski argues that the name Madi-yar developed from Muhammad-i-yar and cannot establish such a relationship. The source also records genetic research concerning the frequency of Y-DNA Haplogroup G-M201 among Madiyars.
