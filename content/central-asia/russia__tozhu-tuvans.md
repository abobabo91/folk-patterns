## Overview

The Tozhu Tuvans, also called Tozhu Tuvinians, Todzhan Tyvans or Todzhinians, are a subgroup of the Tuvans living in Todzhinsky District of the Republic of Tuva. Their own names include *Тугалар* Tugalar and *Тухалар* Tukhalar. They are reindeer herders.

## Language & religion

The language of the Tozhu Tyvan people is a subdialect of the Eastern, or Northeastern, dialect of the Tyvan language. The Tozhu Tyvan dialect is classified within the Taiga Sayan Turkic branch of Sayan Turkic, together with Tere-Khöl Tuvan; most other Tyvan dialects are classified within the Steppe Sayan Turkic branch.
