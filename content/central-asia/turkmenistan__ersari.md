## Overview

Ersari, also called *Ärsary*, are one of the major tribes of the Turkmen people of Central Asia and one of the five major tribes of Turkmenistan. They live mainly in Turkmenistan, Afghanistan and Pakistan, with an overall population of approximately 2.1 million people. The sources identify four sub-tribal divisions: Gara, Bekeul, Gunesh and Uludepe. They are described as direct descendants of the Oghuz Turks and as connected with the Salur tribe, the Seljuk Turkomans, and the Sayin Khan Turkmen tribal confederacy.

## Festivals & rituals

The historical source describes Ersari baba as the legendary leader of the Ersari people and of all Turkmen, and as a figure associated with the consolidation of Turkmen tribes.

## Oral tradition & literature

The source identifies Ersari baba as the legendary leader of the Ersari people and of all Turkmen. It states that he lived in the 13-14th centuries in Mangishlaq (Mangystau Province) and the Balkan mountains (Balkan Province), founded the Sayin Khan Turkmen confederation, and was considered the father of the Ersari people.

## Language & religion

The source gives the name *Ärsary* as a Turkmen form and explains the components as “brave man, master” and “light, bright, yellow.”
