## Overview

The Chahars are a subgroup of Mongols who speak Chakhar Mongolian and predominantly live in southeastern Inner Mongolia, China. Their recorded history includes movements from Shaanxi to the southeastern region controlled by the Northern Yuan dynasty in the 15th century, an eastward movement toward the Liao River in the middle of the 16th century, and later reorganization under Qing rule. They became a tumen of six tumen Mongols under Dayan Khan and were led by his successors, becoming the personal appanage of the Northern Yuan monarchs. After the Revolt of the Three Feudatories, the Chahar Mongols were reorganized into the Eight Banners of Chahar and moved to the area around Zhangjiakou.

## Language & religion

The Chahars speak Chakhar Mongolian.
