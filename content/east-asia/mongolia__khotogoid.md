## Overview

The Khotogoid, also called Khotgoid, are a subgroup of the Mongols living in northwestern Mongolia. Their territory lies roughly between Uvs Lake to the west and the Delgermörön river to the east. They belong to northwestern Khalkha and were one of the major groups that make up Khalkha. They record instead the Khotogoid’s political history, territorial organization, movements, and relationship with neighboring Khalkhas and Oirats.

## Language & religion

The sources identify the Khotogoid as a subgroup of the Mongols and state that they belong to northwestern Khalkha.
