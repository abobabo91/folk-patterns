## Overview

The Üzemchin, also written Ujumchin, Ujumucin, or Ujimqin, are a subgroup of Mongols in eastern Mongolia and Inner Mongolia. They are settled mainly in Sergelen, Bayantu'men, and Choibalsan city of Dornod Province, and in the Xilin Gol League of Inner Mongolia. Some Üzemchins in Mongolia migrated from Xilin Gol immediately after China was freed from the Japanese in 1945. The group was included in the Chahar tumen, one of the six tumen of eastern Mongols in the Northern Yuan Dynasty. The name is probably connected with *üzem*, meaning “grape” or “raisin,” and interpreted as “grape pickers/collectors.”

## Language & religion

The Üzemchin language is a dialect of Chakhar Mongolian.
