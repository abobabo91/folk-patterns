## Overview

The Bonin Islanders, also known as the Ogasawara Islanders or *Ōbeikei tōmin* (“European–American Islanders” in Japanese), are a Euronesian ethnic group native to the Bonin Islands, also called the Ogasawara Islands. They are culturally and genetically distinct from the Yamato, Ainu, and Ryukyuans. The group descends from Europeans, White Americans, Polynesians, and Kanaks who settled Hahajima and Chichijima in the 19th century. The sources describe the Bonin Islanders through their settlement history and surnames.
