## Overview

Astrakhan Tatars are an ethnic subgroup of the Tatars. From the 15th to the 17th centuries, they inhabited the Astrakhan Khanate, dated 1459–1556, which was also inhabited by the Nogai Horde. They live mostly in Astrakhan Oblast, and their population is around 60,000. Since the 17th century, interaction and ethnic mixing with Volga Tatars have increased, while differences between Astrakhan Tatars and Volga Tatars in Astrakhan Oblast have been disappearing. Their speech is described as the mixed Astrakhan (Ästerxan) Tatar dialect; around 43,000 have assimilated to the Middle, or Kazan, dialect. Their ancestors are identified as Kipchaks, Khazars, and some Volga Bulgars. The sources provide demographic, historical, and linguistic information.

## Foodways

The sources provide only a historical description from the *Encyclopædia Britannica Eleventh Edition*, published in 1911, which characterized the Astrakhan Tatars as agriculturists and gardeners.

## Language & religion

Astrakhan (Ästerxan) Tatar is described as a mixed dialect. Around 43,000 speakers have assimilated to the Middle, or Kazan, dialect. The sources identify Kipchaks, Khazars, and some Volga Bulgars among the ancestors of the Astrakhan Tatars, and state that Volga Bulgars had trade colonies in modern Astrakhan and Volgograd oblasts of Russia.
