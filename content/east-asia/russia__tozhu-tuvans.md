## Overview

The Tozhu Tuvans, also called Tozhu Tuvinians, Todzhan Tyvans or Todzhinians, are a subgroup of the Tuvans living in Todzhinsky District of the Republic of Tuva. Their own names include *Тугалар* (Tugalar) and *Тухалар* (Tukhalar). They are reindeer herders. The Tozhu Tyvan language is a subdialect of the Eastern, or Northeastern, dialect of the Tyvan language. It belongs to the Taiga Sayan Turkic branch of Sayan Turkic, together with Tere-Khöl Tuvan, while most other Tyvan dialects belong to the Steppe Sayan Turkic branch.
