## Overview

The Nogais are a Turkic people who speak Nogai, a language of the Kipchak branch of the Turkic languages. They live in Eastern Europe, including the North Caucasus and the Volga region, as well as in Central Asia and Turkey. Most are found in northern Dagestan and Stavropol Krai, with additional populations in Karachay-Cherkessia, Chechnya, and Astrakhan Oblast. Nogais also live in Dobruja, Bulgaria, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Ukraine, Syria, and Jordan. They descend from Mongolic and Turkic tribes that formed the Nogai Horde. In the 1990s, 65,000 were still living in the Northern Caucasus.

## Music & performance

The sources identify Arslanbek Sultanbekov as a Nogai musician.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources do describe seasonal migration connected with animal herding. Nogais raised various herds and migrated seasonally in search of better pastures, and they were proud of nomadic traditions and independence, which they considered superior to settled agricultural life. This information concerns pastoral practice rather than a documented ritual calendar.

## Foodways

Nogais in Turkey have maintained a cuisine including *üken börek*, *kaşık börek*, *tabak börek*, *şır börek*, *köbete*, and *Nogay şay*. The source describes Nogay şay as a drink prepared by boiling milk and tea together with butter, salt, and pepper. These foods are documented in connection with Nogais in Turkey, particularly villages of Central Anatolia around Salt Lake, Eskişehir, and Ceyhan.

## Oral tradition & literature

 The source does state that Nogai ethnic history includes customary identification of clan-family signs called *tamga*, and that the ethnic history of the people is based partly on the naming of a branched clan stratification.

## Language & religion

Nogais speak Nogai, a language from the Kipchak branch of the Turkic languages. The sources state that the Nogai language is still spoken in some villages of Central Anatolia, mainly around Salt Lake, Eskişehir, and Ceyhan. They also state that Nogais are descendants of various Mongolic and Turkic tribes that formed the Nogai Horde.
