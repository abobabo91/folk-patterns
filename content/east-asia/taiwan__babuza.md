## Overview

The Babuza (Chinese: 巴布薩族; pinyin: Bābùsàzú) are a Taiwanese aboriginal people living primarily in Changhua County and around the western part of Taiwan’s Central Basin. They are included within the Plains indigenous people, or Pingpu people, group. Taiwan does not officially recognize the Babuza independently, despite calls for recognition.

## Oral tradition & literature

The sources do mention the picture book *Osubalaki, Balalong Ramut*, identified as the first commercial publication written in Taokas and published in 2020.

## Language & religion

Babuza is a Formosan language of the Babuza and Taokas, indigenous peoples of Taiwan. It is related to or perhaps descended from Favorlang, which is attested from the 17th century. Babuza was once spoken along much of Taiwan’s western coast. Its two rather divergent dialects, Poavosa and the extinct Taokas, were separated by Papora and Pazeh.
