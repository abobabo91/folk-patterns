## Overview

The Olots are an Oirat sub-ethnic group of Choros origin and were one of the strongest tribes of the Oirats. Mongolian Olots live in Erdenebüren and Ölziit sums. There are a few Olots in the Hulunbuir region and around 40,000 Olots in Xinjiang province of China. Ölziit Olots were absorbed into the Khalkha Mongols, while the Erdenebüren Olots, numbering around 3,000, retained their Oirat heritage.
