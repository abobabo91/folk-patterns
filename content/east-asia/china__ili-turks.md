## Overview

The Ili Turks are a Turkic ethnic group native to Ili Kazakh Autonomous Prefecture in northern Xinjiang, China. They speak the Ili Turki language, which belongs to the Karluk branch of the Turkic languages. According to their oral history, they came from the Ferghana Valley of Uzbekistan. The Chinese government does not recognize the Ili Turks as a distinct ethnic group in censuses. They are nomadic herders, and their language contains many unique animal husbandry words.
