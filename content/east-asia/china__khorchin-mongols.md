## Overview

The Khorchin Mongols are a subgroup of the Mongols who speak the Khorchin dialect of Mongolian and predominantly live in northeastern Inner Mongolia of China. The source describes their historical connection with the Borjigin princes descended from Genghis Khan’s brothers and identifies the Fuyu Guard as their direct ancestral line. It records their movement and political activity around the Nen and Onon rivers, the Khingan mountains, and the Sungari, followed by their submission to the Qing dynasty in 1624. The Khorchins were the first Mongol tribe to submit to the Qing dynasty and produced fermented mare’s milk for Manchu emperors.

## Festivals & rituals

 The historical source records that the Khorchins produced fermented mare’s milk for Manchu emperors.

## Foodways

The sources identify fermented mare’s milk as a product made by the Khorchins for Manchu emperors.

## Language & religion

The Khorchin speak the Khorchin dialect of Mongolian.
