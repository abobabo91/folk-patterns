## Overview

The Dörbets, known in English as The Fours, are the second largest subgroup of Mongol people in modern Mongolia and were formerly one of the major tribes of the Four Oirat confederation in the 15th–18th centuries. They are distributed among the western provinces of Mongolia, Kalmykia, and a small portion of Heilongjiang, China; in modern-day Mongolia, they are centered in Uvs Province. The Dörbets in Mongolia numbered 55,200 in 1989 and 66,706 in 2000. The sources describe their historical political affiliations, migrations, and settlement history.
