## Overview

The Dariganga are an eastern Mongol subgroup in Mongolia who mainly live in Dari Ovoo and Ganga Lake, in Sukhbaatar Province. The sources identify them as a Mongol subgroup. It is believed that the Qing Dynasty resettled the Dariganga from Chahar, Khalkha, and Ööled to herd horses of the Emperor in the late 1690s. Since 1691, after changes in Mongolian administrative structures, they have lived in the sums of Dariganga, Naran, Ongon, Khalzan, Asgat, and Bayandelger in Sukhbaatar Province.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources record administrative and historical changes affecting the Dariganga. From 1912 on, a Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Boghda Khaanate of Mongolia supervised them, and in 1921 the People’s Republic of Mongolia changed their banner system.

## Language & religion

The sources identify the Dariganga as an eastern Mongol subgroup and give the Mongolian form Дарьганга.
