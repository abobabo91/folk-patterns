## Overview

The Chulyms, also called Chulym Tatars or Tom Karagas, are a Turkic people living in the Tomsk Oblast and Krasnoyarsk Krai in Russia. They traditionally lived along the middle and lower reaches of the Chulym River, a tributary of the Ob River. In 2021, there were 382 Chulyms in Russia. They speak Chulym and identify themselves as Chulymian Tatars. Their history developed through the mixing of Turkic groups, including partially Teleuts, Yenisei Kyrgyz and groups of Siberian Tatars. Their folk-cultural profile is especially connected in the sources with hunting, trapping, changing forms of livelihood, and a syncretic religious tradition combining Russian Orthodoxy and Shamanism.

## Festivals & rituals

In 1720, the Chulyms were forcefully converted to Christianity.

## Language & religion

Chulym is a Turkic language spoken by the Chulyms. Their religious life is described as a syncretic form of Russian Orthodoxy and Shamanism.
