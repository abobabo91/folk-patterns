## Overview

The Zanaki are a Bantu ethnolinguistic group from the heart of Mara Region, Tanzania, east of Lake Victoria. The group is subdivided into the Birus and the Buturis. Zanaki, also called *Ikizanaki*, is a Bantu language of Tanzania spoken by the Zanaki people of Musoma. The sources identify Julius Nyerere as a Zanaki and describe Zanaki through their language, regional location, subdivisions, and notable public figures.

## Language & religion

Zanaki, or *Ikizanaki*, is identified as a Bantu language of Tanzania spoken by the Zanaki people of Musoma. The sources state that it was the first language of Tanzanian president Julius Nyerere.
