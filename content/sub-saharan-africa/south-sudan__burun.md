## Overview

The Burun are a Luo Nilotic group from South Sudan. They live around the Upper Nile Valley, especially in Dajo, Pacime, Waldese, and Kigile. The 2008 census counted about 8,000 Burun people. The sources mainly record their language and museum-held weapons and objects.

## Material culture

The documented material culture consists mainly of weapons, a bow, arrows, a club, and head-rests.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

Museum records document Burun-associated weapons, a bow, arrows, a club, and head-rests.

- **Iron throwing knife** — An iron throwing knife has an unusual shape.
- **Barbed spear head** — A spear head has a chased design and backwards-pointing barbs, and is attached to a wooden shaft.
- **Metal-headed spear** — A spear has a metal head, a thin light wooden shaft, a metal sleeve, and a band of commercial tape.
- **Wooden club** — A wooden club has a flat head and a handle.
- **Bamboo bow and arrows** — A bamboo bow has a string, and the arrows have bamboo shafts with fire-hardened tapering wood heads.

## Language & religion

Burun is a Luo Nilotic language, but the supplied sources do not document Burun religion or writing systems.

- **Burun language** (*Burun*) — Burun is a Luo Nilotic language spoken by the Burun.
- **Northern Burun** — Northern Burun is described as a Nilotic language of Sudan.
- **Three varieties** — Blench (2012) lists three Burun varieties separately.
