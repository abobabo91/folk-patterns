## Material culture

### Clothing & dress

The catalogue records one cap made from cotton cloth that folds flat in four panels. It is embroidered with yellow and pink thread on purple background cloth, has a white lower border and a red and white button at the top, and its materials appear to be of European manufacture.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The museum records describe wooden objects including coffins and model coffins in the forms of an eagle, elephant, catfish, camera, cocoa pod, Coca-Cola bottle, and lager bottle marked “Premium Club.” The eagle coffin has painted feather markings, hinged wings, carrying handles, and a cavity lined with textiles; the elephant coffin has a cavity lined with green silk and red textile. The catfish, cocoa pod, and lager-bottle coffins are painted and have hinged wooden lids over cavities.

### Jewelry & body adornment

One catalogue record describes a wooden phallic fertility figurine of a standing male with a blue bead girdle and necklace, another necklace, and straw around the neck; it is partly covered with white paint.

## Festivals & rituals

The catalogue description of a wooden phallic fertility figurine records the object’s form.
