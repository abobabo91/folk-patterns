## Overview

The Brong are an ethnic group associated with Ghana and Sub-Saharan Africa. The supplied sources give little information about their settlements, population, language, history, or folk culture. Museum records document pottery, bronze objects, and a brass mask gold weight. Most other cultural areas are not described.

## Material culture
