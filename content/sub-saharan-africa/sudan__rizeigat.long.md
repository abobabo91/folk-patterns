## Overview

The Rizeigat, also spelled Rizigat, Rezeigat, and in standard Arabic Rizayqat, are a Muslim Arab tribe residing predominantly in Sudan’s Darfur region and Chad. They belong to the greater Baggara Arab fraternity of Darfur and Chad and are a branch of the Juhayanah group. They speak Sudanese and Chadian Arabic. The Rizeigat are primarily nomadic herders whose journeys depend on the seasons of the year. They are divided into the Abbala, or camel-herding Rizeigat, of northern Darfur and Chad, and the Baggara, who inhabit south-east Darfur. Their social and political history includes pastoral relations with settled farmers, participation in armed conflicts, and the formation of the Murahileen, or mounted raiders.
