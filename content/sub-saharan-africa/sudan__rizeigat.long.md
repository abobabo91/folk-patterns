## Overview

The Rizeigat, also spelled Rizigat, Rezeigat, and in standard Arabic Rizayqat, are a Muslim Arab tribe residing predominantly in Sudan’s Darfur region and Chad. They belong to the greater Baggara Arab fraternity of Darfur and Chad and are a branch of the Juhayanah group. They speak Sudanese and Chadian Arabic. The Rizeigat are primarily nomadic herders whose journeys depend on the seasons of the year. They are divided into the Abbala, or camel-herding Rizeigat, of northern Darfur and Chad, and the Baggara, who inhabit south-east Darfur. Their social and political history includes pastoral relations with settled farmers, participation in armed conflicts, and the formation of the Murahileen, or mounted raiders.

## Material culture

### Architecture

The Northern Rizeigat possess no Dar, or tribal homeland, in Darfur. Instead, they have Damra, described as temporary settlements allocated to Arab nomads within land under the jurisdiction of other tribes.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The museum catalogue records objects associated with the material culture shown for this profile. They include baskets with lids made of vegetable fibre and leather, a basket described as marriage equipment for a wedding house, a glass bottle covered with leather, and a basket-and-lid set with leather tassels hanging from each side and red triangular leather decoration around the rim.

## Music & performance

The sources state that the Rizeigat speak Sudanese and Chadian Arabic, but provide no information about oral performance or music.

## Festivals & rituals

One museum catalogue record identifies a basket as marriage equipment for a wedding house, but gives no further account of marriage practices.

## Foodways

The Wikipedia article states that the Rizeigat are herders and that camel ownership is central to status and identity.

## Oral tradition & literature

The sources do record historical accounts involving named political and military figures, including Madibbo Ali, Slatin Pasha, Musa Hilal, and Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo.

## Language & religion

The Rizeigat are described as a Muslim Arab tribe. They speak both Sudanese and Chadian Arabic. The sources identify them with the greater Baggara Arab fraternity and describe them as a branch of the Juhayanah group.
