## Overview

The Zinza are a Bantu ethnolinguistic group living on the southwestern islands of Lake Victoria and the lakeshore of northwestern Tanzania. Their language, Zinza (also called Luzinza), has been documented through lexicons and grammatical studies. The primary material record comes from British Museum objects registered in 1950: plaited grass containers, head cushions, decorated calabashes, and smoking pipes.

## Material culture

Zinza material culture survives chiefly through plaited grass work in black and white, decorated gourds, and practical objects for storage and personal use.

### Textile & pattern traditions

The Zinza's clearest design tradition is black-and-white plaited grass work, with patterning carried in the plaiting itself.

- **Black-and-white plaited grass baskets** — Grass baskets patterned in black and white with matching plaited lids, where color is created through the plaiting technique itself.
- **Twilled head cushion** — A circular plaited head-ring in black and white worked with twilled plait to create a diagonally striped effect.
- **Incised calabash ornament** — A yellow calabash decorated with fine burnt incision on the gourd wall.

## Language & religion

Zinza is a Bantu language spoken on the southern shore of Lake Victoria, documented in lexicons and grammatical studies; no religious practices are recorded.

- **Luzinza** — The language's own name for itself, a Bantu language of Tanzania.
- **Wazinza** — The Swahili name for the Zinza people.

## Glossary

- *Luzinza* — The Zinza language's own name for itself
- *Wazinza* — The Swahili name for the Zinza people
- *Zinza* — A Bantu ethnolinguistic group of northwestern Tanzania
- *plaited grass* — Woven grass technique used for baskets and cushions
- *twilled plait* — A diagonal-weave plaiting technique creating stripe patterns
- *calabash* — A yellow gourd vessel used for storage or serving
- *head-ring* — A circular woven pad placed on the head to carry loads
- *soapstone* — Black stone used for the bowl of a tobacco pipe
- *snuff* — Powdered tobacco inhaled through the nose
- *Bantu* — A major language family of sub-Saharan Africa
- *Lake Victoria* — The large lake on Tanzania's northwestern border where Zinza live
- *lexicon* — A comprehensive list of words and their meanings in a language
