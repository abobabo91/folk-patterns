### Clothing & dress

The catalogue records include an openwork woman’s apron made of glass beadwork and shells with a skin fringe, an apron made of cowrie shells and glass beads, pointed caps made of coloured straws, and a conical basketry hat with green and navy blue bands and yellow and red decorations; they do not describe everyday or ceremonial dress more broadly.

### Architecture
