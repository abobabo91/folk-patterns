## Overview

The Bedick, also called Bedik, are a minority ethnic group of Africa. They live in villages in the most isolated area of eastern Senegal, in the Arrondissement of Bandafassi; Iwol is one of their settlements. They speak the Bedik language. Their religion combines animist roots with more recent Christian influence. Their connections with Guinea Conakry and Mali are described as stronger than those with Senegal, and they have contact with Bassari and Serer groups. Their ancestors are identified as the families Keita and Camera, who came from Mali because of the war led by Alpha Yaye, coming from Fouta Djalon.
