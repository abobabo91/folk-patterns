### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The museum catalogue records several objects with iron, wood, brass wire, and brass nails. These include a dagger with an iron blade and wooden sheath bound with brass wire; a knife with an iron blade in a black-wood sheath bound with brass wire; an axe with an iron blade and a knob-headed handle covered with brass binding; a sword or long knife with a two-edged iron blade and a handle covered with brass wire; and another axe with a flat iron blade and dark-wood handle bound with brass wire. The catalogue also lists a knife with an iron blade and wooden sheath, an axe with a wooden handle through which an iron blade passes, and a “Despatch box” with a hinged lid, brass nails, and a handle terminating in the shape of a gun stock.
