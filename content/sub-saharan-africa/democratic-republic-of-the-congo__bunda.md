## Overview

The Bunda people, also known as Ambuun and Mbuun, are an ethnic group who mostly live in Idiofa Territory of Kwilu Province in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. They remain the most important group in the center of Idiofa territory and form the majority of the population of the administrative center of Idiofa. Bunda populations are also found in Bulungu Territory and Gungu Territory. They speak the Mpuono language, which was spoken by an estimated 165,000 people as of 1972. The Belgian colonial administration originally named Idiofa Territory “Babunda Territory” after the Bunda people, who formed the majority of its population. The available source presents the Bunda chiefly through their regional distribution, language, and subsistence activities.

## Foodways

The Bunda territory is described as having a tropical climate with alternating dry and rainy seasons. Rainy-season precipitation is so intense that it creates a major obstacle to farming, and food may be scarce during that period. Hunting and fishing are individual activities in the rainy season. Food is abundant in the dry season, which is the principal season for communal hunting and fishing.
