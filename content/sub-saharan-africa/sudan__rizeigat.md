## Overview

The Rizeigat are a Muslim Arab tribe in Darfur and Chad. They are part of the Baggara Arab fraternity and a branch of the Juhayanah group. Many are nomadic herders whose movements follow the seasons. The Abbala herd camels in northern Darfur and Chad, while the Baggara live in south-east Darfur.

### Architecture

Northern Rizeigat use temporary settlements called Damra rather than a tribal homeland called Dar in Darfur.

- **Temporary nomad settlement** (*Damra*) — A Damra is a temporary settlement allocated to Arab nomads on land under another tribe’s jurisdiction.
- **Tribal homeland** (*Dar*) — The Northern Rizeigat are described as having no Dar, or tribal homeland, in Darfur.

## Language & religion

The Rizeigat are Muslim Arabs who speak Sudanese and Chadian Arabic.

- **Muslim Arab tribe** (*Rizeigat*) — The Rizeigat are described as a Muslim Arab tribe.
- **Sudanese Arabic** — The Rizeigat speak Sudanese Arabic.
- **Chadian Arabic** — The Rizeigat speak Chadian Arabic.
- **Baggara Arab fraternity** (*Baggara*) — The Rizeigat belong to the greater Baggara Arab fraternity of Darfur and Chad.
- **Juhayanah branch** (*Juhayanah*) — The Rizeigat are described as a branch of the Juhayanah group.
