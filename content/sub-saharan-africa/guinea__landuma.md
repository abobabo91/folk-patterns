## Overview

The Landuma, also called Landoma, Landima, Landouma, Cocoli, Kokoli, Tiapi, or Tyapi, are an African people who live primarily in Guinea, in the area of the Upper Nunez. They have been described as “pre-Mandingas” because they settled in the region before the arrival of the Mande people. Walter Rodney places them alongside the Nalu, Baga, and Temne peoples.

## Language & religion

Landoma, also called Landouman, Landuma, or Cocoli (*Cikogoli*), is a language of western Guinea. It is the most populous of the Baga languages and belongs to the Mel branch of the Niger–Congo language family; it is related to Temne of Sierra Leone. The sources describe two varieties, Landoma (Landuma) and Cikogoli (Cocoli). Cikogoli is being replaced by Fula. The language is also known by the names Tiapi, Tyapi, Tyopi, and Tapessi; Wilson reports that this is a derogatory name.
