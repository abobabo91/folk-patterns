### Clothing & dress

The records show decorated aprons, straw caps, and a conical basketry hat.

- **Openwork woman’s apron** — This apron is made of glass beadwork and shells with a skin fringe.
- **Cowrie-shell apron** — This apron is made of cowrie shells and glass beads.
- **Pointed straw caps** — These caps are made of coloured straws.
- **Conical basketry hat** — This hat has green and navy blue bands with yellow and red decorations.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The records include a hide shield, a spear, gourd containers, and several kinds of baskets.

- **Hippopotamus-hide shield** — The shield has a central silver boss, silver plates in the rim, and eight leather pockets containing charms.
- **Spear or lance** — The weapon has an iron head and a wooden shaft.
- **Beaded gourd containers** — The gourd containers are decorated with beads or covered with a network of beads.
- **Leather-strip and fibre baskets** — These baskets are made from leather strips and fibre.
- **Coiled grass or fibre baskets** — These baskets are flat or conical and are formed of coiled grass or fibre.
