## Overview

The Mbukushu are a Bantu-speaking group linked to the larger Lozi ethnic group. They live mainly along the Kavango River and around the Okavango Delta. Their settlements depend on fishing, farming, and livestock keeping. In Namibia, they hold traditional authority and are one of the five Kavango Kingdoms.
