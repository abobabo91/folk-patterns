## Overview

The Samo, also known as Sanan, are a sub-ethnic group of the Mandinka people from West Africa. They mainly live in northwest Burkina Faso and across the border in southern Mali.

## Language & religion

The sources identify the Samo language as a subject associated with Burkina Faso and distinguish it from Samo language traditions in New Guinea.
