## Overview

The supplied sources identify the Kololo as a tribe in Zambia. They give no population estimate, settlement area, history, language family, or religious affiliation. Most cultural details are not documented. A museum catalogue records several iron weapons and other objects, along with terms connected with Kololo.

## Material culture

The documented material culture consists mainly of iron weapons and wooden objects with brass wire, brass binding, or brass nails.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The catalogue records iron weapons and a brass-nailed box, but gives no information about their use or origin.

- **Sword or long knife** — The sword or long knife has a two-edged iron blade and a handle covered with brass wire.
- **Despatch box** — The box has a hinged lid, brass nails, and a handle shaped like a gun stock.
