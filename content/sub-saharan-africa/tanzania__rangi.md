## Overview

The Rangi live in central Tanzania and speak a Bantu language. Their culture reflects both Bantu and Cushitic heritage and contact with surrounding peoples. Their history has different accounts, dating from about 300 AD to around 1500–1700. The profile gives its clearest details about language, ceremonies, and museum-recorded objects.

## Material culture

The documented material culture includes wooden and iron weapons, a milking pail, and a horn bugle, while many other areas are not described.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

Museum records describe Rangi weapons, a milking pail, and a horn bugle.

- **Wooden spear with iron point** — The museum catalogue records a wooden spear fitted with an iron point.
- **Wood-and-iron arrow** — The catalogue records arrows made of wood and iron.
- **Poisoned arrow** — The catalogue records poisoned arrows made from wood, sinew, and feathers.
- **Iron bill-hook** — The catalogue records an iron bill-hook with a wooden handle.
- **Horn bugle** — The catalogue records a bugle made from a horn.

## Music & performance

## Festivals & rituals

The sources name two ceremonies.

- **Blessing the Year** — This is a Wasi/Rangi ceremony, but the profile gives no details about its date, participants, or meaning.
- **Blessing a New Grove** — This is a Rangi circumcision ceremony, but the profile gives no details about its proceedings or ritual meaning.

## Language & religion

Rangi is a Bantu language with two mutually intelligible varieties, while Rangi religious beliefs are not documented.

- **Rangi language** (*Rangi; Langi; Kilangi; Kɨlaangi; Kilaangi*) — The language is spoken in Kondoa District and is written in the Latin alphabet with ɨ and ʉ.
- **Swahili language name** (*Kirangi*) — Kirangi is the common and dominant Swahili name for the Rangi language.
- **Highlands variety** (*Irangi ya Juu*) — Irangi ya Juu is the Rangi Highlands variety.
- **Lowlands variety** (*Irangi ya Chini*) — Irangi ya Chini is the Rangi Lowlands variety.
- **Rangi noun-class system** (*ɨ; ʉ; ch; ng'; ny; sh*) — Rangi has 19 noun classes and uses these letters and letter combinations in writing.
