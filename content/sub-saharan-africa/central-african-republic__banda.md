## Overview

The Banda traditionally lived in dispersed home groups led by a headman. They hunted, fished, gathered wild foods, and grew crops. Their cultural record includes carved wooden objects, animal-shaped slit drums, wooden pipes, and rites of passage. Christianity is common, but traditional religious values and practices continue.

## Music & performance

Banda music uses animal-shaped slit drums and, among the Banda-Linda, wooden pipes.

- **Animal-shaped slit drums** (*Banda-Yangere*) — These drums served musical and communicative functions in celebrations and message sending.
- **Wooden pipes** (*Banda-Linda Horns*) — The Banda-Linda are known for music using wooden pipes called Banda-Linda Horns.

## Festivals & rituals

Banda rites include the Semali adulthood rite and offerings to ancestral spirits for crop success.

- **Adulthood rite** (*Semali*) — Semali recognizes the crossing into adulthood.
- **Crop-success offerings** — People make sacrificial offerings to ancestral spirits for seasonal success of crops.
- **Bridewealth** — Traditional bridewealth at weddings included iron implements for the family.
- **Polygyny** — Polygyny was practiced historically but has declined in modern times.

## Foodways

The Banda traditionally sustain themselves through hunting, fishing, gathering wild foods, and crop farming.

- **Hunting** — Hunting is one way the Banda traditionally obtained food.
- **Fishing** — Fishing is one way the Banda traditionally obtained food.
- **Gathering wild foods** — The Banda traditionally gathered wild foods.
- **Crop farming** — The Banda grow crops and are settled farmers in the savannas.
- **Cotton and cassava farming** — French colonial officials promoted cotton and cassava farming among the Banda.

## Language & religion

The Banda speak Banda or Ubangian languages and combine Christianity with continuing traditional beliefs.

- **Banda languages** (*Banda*) — The Banda speak languages in the Niger-Congo family known as Banda or Ubangian languages.
- **Regional vernaculars** (*vernaculars*) — Nine distinct vernaculars are distributed across different areas.
- **Christianity** (*Protestants; Catholics*) — Most Banda are Protestants or Catholics, with 52% Protestant and 38% Catholic.
- **Traditional religious systems** (*ancestral spirits*) — Traditional beliefs continue alongside Christianity, including offerings to ancestral spirits for crop success.
- **War chiefs** — During crises, the Banda selected war chiefs to resist slave raids and respond to wars, and their powers ended afterward.
