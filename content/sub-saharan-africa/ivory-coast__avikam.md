## Overview

The Avikam are an Akan people who live predominantly in Ivory Coast. The Avikam people of southern Cote d'Ivoire live in a tropical rainforest fringed by a coastal lagoon. Many also live in Abidjan, the country's economic capital. Avikam is one of the Lagoon languages of Ivory Coast and is spoken in Grand Lahou Département, Avikam Canton, South Department. It is a Kwa language closely related to Alladian, although its wider position is unclear.

## Festivals & rituals

About a century ago, missionaries from Africa and Europe delivered the Gospel to the Avikam people. Today, a sizable portion claim to be Christians while continuing to adhere to strongly established animistic customs. Strong believers experience daily pressure from individuals who insist on employing fetishes, described in the source as things produced by humans and thought to possess supernatural powers, to fend off evil spirits.

## Foodways

Most Avikam people earn a living from agriculture. Some are subsistence farmers who live off what they raise in their own fields. Others work in agricultural cooperatives that grow palm oil, coconut, cassava, or bananas, while others work for large producers of rubber and palm oil.
