### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The museum records document pottery and bronze objects. They include a pottery vessel with a round body and flared rim, a tall pottery pot with four looped handles on either side and a band of vertical lines around its middle, and a pottery vessel with a narrow neck. The records also describe a bronze mask with an elongated face and slits at the eyes and mouth, a bronze pedestal vase with handles and a cover bearing engraved decoration, a bronze lamp with a bowl and stem standing on four feet, a bronze tripod vessel with three legs and a bird and dragon on the lip, and a bronze lamp shaped like a bird with a chain for suspension.
