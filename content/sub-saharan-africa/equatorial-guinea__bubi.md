## Overview

The Bubi are a Bantu people indigenous to Bioko Island in Equatorial Guinea. Their language came from continental Africa more than three thousand years ago and has several regional forms. Bubi spiritual beliefs include a creator god, spirits linked to natural landmarks, and overlapping spiritual and material worlds. Museum records preserve a small group of shell adornments, baskets and an earthenware bowl.

## Material culture

The documented material culture consists mainly of shell adornments, feathered ornaments, lidded baskets and one earthenware bowl.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources do not document a festival calendar or named life-cycle rites, but they describe a traditional spiritual world.

- **Labako-ppua** — Labako-ppua was understood as heaven and the place of the angels.
- **Ommo ich'ori** — Ommo ich'ori was understood as hell and the place of the bad angels.
- **Ommo boeboe** — Ommo boeboe was understood as limbo.
- **Rupe** — Rupe was the head god who created and cared for the world.
- **Eri** — Eri was the southern regional name for the head god Rupe.

## Foodways

The sources do not document Bubi foods, cooking, dishes or farming, but one basket carried palm-oil berries.

- **Palm-oil berries** — A small lidded basket was recorded as being used to carry palm-oil berries.

## Oral tradition & literature

The sources do not record Bubi epics, folktales, proverbs or named storytelling forms, but they mention folklore about migration.

- **Migration folklore** — Indigenous Bubi folklore says that the tribe came to Bioko Island about 3,000 years ago to escape servitude.

## Language & religion

Bube is a tonal Bantu language with regional dialects, and Bubi spirituality combines Catholicism with older beliefs.

- **Bube** — Bube is the Bubi language, spoken mainly by the Bubi and also in Gabon and Cameroon.
- **Bohobé** — Bohobé is another name for the Bube language.
- **Bube–Benga** — Bube–Benga is another name for the Bube language.
- **Etyö** — Etyö is another name for the Bube language.
- **Rebola** — Rebola is one of the Bube dialects in the North.
