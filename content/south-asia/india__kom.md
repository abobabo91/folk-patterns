## Overview

The Kom are a Tibeto-Burmese tribe mainly found in Manipur, in northeastern India. Their clans include Karong, Serto, Leivon, Mangte and Telien. They speak Kom natively, a Tibeto-Burman language, and also use Meitei as a second language. According to the 2001 Census of India, the population of Kom people was 14,602. The sources identify the Kom as an ethnic group and record Mary Kom, an Indian Olympic boxer and politician, as a notable person.

## Language & religion

Kom is described as a Tibeto-Burman language spoken natively by the Kom, who also use Meitei as a second language.
