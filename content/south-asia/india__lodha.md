## Overview

The Lodha people, also honorifically known as Sabar and Savar, are a Scheduled Tribe and particularly vulnerable tribal group of India. They inhabit primarily West Bengal and Odisha. In West Bengal, Lodhas mostly live in the Paschim Medinipur and Jhargham districts. In Odisha, they are primarily found in the Suliapada and Morada blocks of the Baripada sub-division of Mayurbhanj district. As per the 2001 census, Lodhas numbered 84,966 and formed 1.9 per cent of the scheduled tribe population of West Bengal. As per the 2011 census, the Lodhas population is 108,707 and 9,785 in West Bengal and Odisha respectively.

The Wikipedia source states that Lodhas have been the focus of anthropologists and social activists, and identifies empirical studies by researchers from the Department of Anthropology at Vidyasagar University.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources state that a section of the Lodha has converted to Islam and formed a distinct community called the Lodha Muslims, but provide no further account of religious or ritual practice.

## Foodways

The Wikipedia source states that the Lodhas were traditionally dependent upon the forests for a living.

## Oral tradition & literature

The source records the honorific names Sabar and Savar and states that Lodha titles such as Nayek, Mallick, Digar, Sardar, Bhokta, Kotal, Dandapat, and Bhunya were also used by the Sabars.
