## Overview

Kakhetians (*კახელები*, romanized as *k'akhelebi*) are an ethnographic group of Georgians who speak the Kakhetian dialect of the Georgian language. They are the indigenous population of Kakheti, a historical region and fertile valley in eastern Georgia. Kakheti produces much of the country’s wine, which is the principal cultural and economic feature mentioned in the sources. Most Kakhetians adhere to the Georgian Orthodox Church.

## Foodways
