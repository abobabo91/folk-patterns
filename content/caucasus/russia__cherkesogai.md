## Overview

Cherkesogai, also called Circassian Armenians, are ethnic Armenians who have inhabited Circassia since the end of the 15th century. They historically spoke the Adyghe language, although most currently speak Russian as their first language. They reside mostly in Armavir and Maykop, with other communities in the Kuban region and larger towns including Novorossiysk, Anapa and Ekaterinodar. Their total number was estimated at about 50,000 in 2008. The group is distinguished in the source material by its Armenian identity, its historical use of Adyghe, and its settlement history in the North Caucasus.

## Language & religion

Cherkesogai are ethnic Armenians who historically spoke the Adyghe language, in contrast to other Armenians living in the region; currently, most speak Russian as their first language. According to the Russian 2002 Census, 230 Armenians spoke Lowland Adyghe and 222 spoke Kabardian Adyghe natively.
