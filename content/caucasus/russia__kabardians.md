## Overview

The Kabardians, also known by the plural term *Kabarday*, are one of the twelve major Circassian tribes and represent one of the twelve stars on the green-and-gold Circassian flag. Historically, they lived in Kabardia, a region of the north Caucasus. Today, they live mostly in the Russian republic of Kabardino-Balkaria, which partly corresponds to the historic region. They are also present in Karachay-Cherkessia and other countries, including Turkey, Egypt, Jordan, the United States and Israel. Along with the Besleney tribe, they speak a distinctive dialect of Circassian. The sources identify the Kabardians as the largest Circassian group in the world and in Russia, Turkey and Egypt.

## Language & religion

Kabardians speak a distinctive dialect of Circassian, shared with the Besleney tribe. Religions historically practiced by Kabardians include the native Adyghe Xabze faith, Christianity and Islam. The majority had converted to Islam by the early 19th century; most Kabardians are either Non-denominational or Sunni Muslims of the Hanafi school. The source states that 1.8% of Kabardians practice traditional Xabze beliefs in Kabardino-Balkaria. Kabardians also constituted one of the earliest Christian communities in Europe, converting in the late 2nd and early 3rd centuries. Kabardians living in Mozdoksky District in the Republic of North Ossetia–Alania are Orthodox Christians, as are some Kabardians in the southern part of Kursky district of Stavropol Krai. There are also some Roman Catholic Kabardians.
