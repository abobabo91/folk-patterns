## Overview

Montenegrins, or *Crnogorci* / Црногорци, are a South Slavic ethnic group native to Montenegro. Their name is glossed as “People of the Black Mountain.” They share a common culture, ancestry, and history, and speak the Montenegrin language, which is the official language of Montenegro. Outside Montenegro and Europe, Montenegrin diaspora groups are found, for example, in the United States, Canada, Australia, and Argentina; around 600,000 Montenegrin-descended people are estimated to reside outside Montenegro. In 2023, 152,649 Montenegrins both held Montenegrin citizenship and resided outside Montenegro.

## Language & religion

Montenegrins are a South Slavic ethnic group, and the Montenegrin language is the official language of Montenegro. In the 2023 census, 71.4% of ethnic Montenegrins in Montenegro declared Montenegrin as their native language, 22.9% declared Serbian, and 2.9% declared Serbo-Croatian. Montenegrins are mostly Orthodox Christians, although the population also includes Catholics, Muslims, and irreligious people. Most ethnic Montenegrins are Eastern Orthodox.
