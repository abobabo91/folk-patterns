## Overview

The Shokshas are an ethnographic group of Erzya people, named after the village of Shoksha in Tengushevsky District, Mordovia. They live mostly in Mordovia, particularly in Tengushevsky District and Torbeyevsky District. The ethnonym is relatively recent.

## Language & religion

The Shoksha speak the Shoksha dialect, a dialect of the Erzya language formed under the influence of the Moksha language. The language is unwritten and critically endangered.
