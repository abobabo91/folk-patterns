## Overview

The Hungarian Croats (*Hrvati u Mađarskoj*; *Magyarországi horvátok*) are an ethnic minority in Hungary. According to the 2011 census, 26,774 Croats lived in Hungary, representing 0.3% of the population. They belong to several ethnographic subgroups, including *Bunjevci*, *Burgenland Croats*, *Podravina Croats*, *Pomurje Croats*, *Raci Croats*, and *Šokci*. Their communities are located along the Croatian-Hungarian, Austrian-Hungarian, and Serbian-Hungarian borders, mostly in western and southern Hungary and along the Danube, including Budapest. The sources identify the common ethnonym and autonym as *horvátok*.

The Croat Theatre of Pécs is identified among the cultural institutions of the Croats of Hungary.

## Festivals & rituals

The Day of Croats of Hungary (*Dan Hrvata u Mađarskoj*) is celebrated on St. Martin’s Day, 11 November.
