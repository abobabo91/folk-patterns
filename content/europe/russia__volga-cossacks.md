## Overview

The Volga Cossacks, also called the Povolzyhe Cossacks, were free Cossack communities in Russia recorded in sources from the 16th century onward. They inhabited areas along the Volga River. The supplied sources describe their participation in Yermak's conquest of Siberia, their organization as the Volga Cossack Host, and their involvement in Pugachev's Rebellion in 1773–1775.

## Material culture

### Architecture

The sources mention the Tsaritsyn fortified line and Dubovka as the center of the Volga Cossack Host.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources record the Volga Cossacks' participation in Pugachev's Rebellion in 1773–1775 and their relocation in 1770 and 1777.

## Language & religion

The sources identify the Volga Cossacks' Russian name as Волжские казаки and record their presence in Russia.
