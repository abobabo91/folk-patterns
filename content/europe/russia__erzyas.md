## Overview

The Erzyas, also called Erzyans’ or Erzya people, are a Mordvinian Finno-Ugric ethnic group. Their native language is Erzya. They are closely related to, but distinct from, Mokshas; together, they are often called Mordvin peoples, especially by outsiders.

## Oral tradition & literature

The sources identify several people associated with Erzyan descent. Syreś Boläeń is described as a public figure, poet, translator, and half-Erzya; Yakov Grigoshin as a writer; and Mariz Kemal as a writer. Purgaz is identified as a leader.

## Language & religion

The Erzyas are described as a Finno-Ugric ethnic group whose native language is Erzya. The sources give the Erzya forms *эрзят* and *eŕźat*, and the Russian forms *эрзяне* and *erzyane*.
