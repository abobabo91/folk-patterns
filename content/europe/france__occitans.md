## Overview

The Occitans are a Romance ethnic group native to the historical region of Occitania, which includes southern France, northeastern Spain, northwestern Italy and Monaco. They have also been called Gascons, Provençals and Auvergnats. Occitania is their principal area of concentration, although Occitans also live in large urban centres in neighbouring regions, including Lyon, Paris, Turin and Barcelona. Occitan is spoken to varying levels by between 100,000 and 800,000 speakers in southern France and northern Italy. Since 2006, Occitan has been recognized as one of the official languages in Catalonia, an autonomous region of Spain. Occitans also live in Guardia Piemontese, Calabria, as well as Argentina, Mexico, Canada and the United States.
