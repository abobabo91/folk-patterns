## Overview

Masovians, also spelled Mazovians and historically known as Masurians, are an ethnographic group of Polish people originating from Masovia, located mostly within the borders of the Masovian Voivodeship, Poland. They speak the Masovian dialect of Polish. In the Polish census of 2021, 97 people declared Mazovian national identity. The group originates from the Lechitic tribe of Masovians, first referenced in historical records by Nestor the Chronicler in the 11th century.
