## Overview

The Mambai, also called Mambae or Manbae, are the second largest ethnic group after the Tetun Dili people in East Timor. They live from the interior of Dili District to the south coast of the territory, especially in Ainaro and Manufahi. Their principal centers are Ermera, Aileu, Remexio Administrative Post, Turiscai, Maubisse, Ainaro and Same, East Timor. The Mambai number about 80,000. Their language belongs to the Central–Eastern Malayo-Polynesian languages of the Timoric languages branch and is the second most common mother tongue in East Timor, with 195,778 speakers. Circular houses with conical roofs and cultivation of maize, rice and root vegetables are documented features of Mambai life.
