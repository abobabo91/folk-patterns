## Overview

The Thổ ethnic group, also known as Keo, Mon, Cuoi, Ho, and Tay Poong, inhabits the mountainous regions of northern Vietnam, mainly Nghệ An Province southwest of Hanoi. In 2019, the population numbered 91,430. The Thổ are one of the four main groups of Vietic speakers in Vietnam, alongside the Việt, Mường, and Chứt. Thổ is a heterogeneous group consisting of different Vietic peoples, and many Thổ speak languages closely related to Vietnamese. Their culture combines elements of Việt, Mường, and Thái culture. They mainly grow rice and ramie, use hemp to make bags, hammocks, and nets, and live in stilt houses whose forms differ between northern and southern parts of Nghệ An.

## Material culture

### Textile & pattern traditions

**Ramie and hemp work** — The Thổ mainly grow rice and ramie. They use hemp to make bags, hammocks, and nets.

**Purchased Thái clothing** — Many clothing items are purchased from the Thái, forming part of a material culture that combines Vietnamese, Mường, and Thái traditions.

**Vietnamese, Mường, and Tai traditions** — Thổ clothing is described as a mixture of Vietnamese, Muong, and Tai traditions from the early modern period.

### Clothing & dress

Women’s clothing can resemble the *Áo bà ba* or the *Áo tứ thân*, worn with a sarong and a kerchief. Men wear simple tunics and pants. The sources describe Thổ clothing as a mixture of Vietnamese, Muong, and Tai traditions from the early modern period. Many clothing items are purchased from the Thái.

### Architecture

In the northern communes of Nghệ An, Thổ stilt houses are identical to those of the Muong. In the south, houses follow the Thái style.

## Foodways

The sources state that the Thổ mainly grow rice and ramie.

## Language & religion

Thổ languages form a heterogeneous group, and local groups have distinct languages. All Thổ languages belong to the Vietic branch. Nguyễn Hữu Hoành classified Thổ languages into five groups according to their position in the Vietic branch.

*Tày Poọng* and *Đan Lai*, also called Liha, share 85% of their basic lexicon according to lexicostatistical studies. Their speakers reside mainly in Con Cuông and Tương Dương districts, and the language may have a close relation with Hung language, also called Toum, in Laos. *Cuối* is described as a distinct language from Vietnamese, Mường, and Poọng-Đan Lai. Its dialects include *Cuối Chăm* in Tân Hợp commune, Tân Kỳ district, and *Cuối Nếp*, also called *Cuối Làng Lỡ*, in Thái Hòa town.

*Mọn* and *Họ* share 98% of their basic lexicon and are closer to Mường languages than Cuối and Poọng. *Kẹo* shares 99% of its lexicon with the Nghệ An dialect of Vietnamese. *Thổ Lâm La* and *Thổ Như Xuân* share 94% and 95% of their basic lexicon with the Nghệ An dialect.
