## Overview

The Thái are Tai peoples who mainly live in Vietnam’s Northwest Region. Their main groups are the Black Tai, White Tai, and Red Tai; Tai Lue are classified separately as Lự. They settled in river valleys and cultivated rice. Their historical center was Muang Thaeng, linked with today’s Điện Biên Phủ.

The historical account links Muang Thaeng with the legend of Khun Borom.

## Language & religion

The Thái speak Tai languages, use the Tai Viet script, and are mostly linked with the Chiang Saen branch of Southwestern Tai languages.

- **Tai Viet script** (*Tai Viet*) — The Thái use the Tai Viet script.
- **Chiang Saen branch** (*Chiang Saen*) — Most Thái languages in Vietnam belong to the Chiang Saen branch of Southwestern Tai languages.
- **Black Tai** — Black Tai is one of the principal Thái groups.
- **White Tai** — White Tai is one of the principal Thái groups.
- **Red Tai** — Red Tai is one of the principal Thái groups.
