## Overview

The Ukit, also known as *Bhuket*, are a small minority tribe found in Sarawak, Malaysia. Until recently, they were nomads in the rain forests of Borneo. Some were settled at Rumah Ukit on the upper reaches of the Batang Balui, or Balui River. Most are found in the Upper Rajom and Tatau rivers and in Baleh, Sarawak. The Ukit population is estimated to be only about 120, although an unknown number may continue to live as forest nomads. Their population has declined through killings in conflict, displacement, and intermarriage with other tribes. They were also regarded as a sub-group of the purported Klemantan people. Ukit is a Punan language of Sarawak, Malaysia, while *Punan Ukit* is a dialect of the related language Bukitan.
