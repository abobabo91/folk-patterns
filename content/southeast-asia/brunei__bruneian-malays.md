## Overview

Bruneian Malays (*Orang Melayu Brunei*) are a native Malay ethnic group living in Brunei, the federal territory of Labuan, the southwestern coast of Sabah, and the northern parts of Sarawak. They are a subgroup of the wider Malay population of the Malay world and are ethnically related to Malays in West Malaysia, Sarawak, Singapore, Indonesia, and southern Thailand, while differing particularly in language and culture. They state that Bruneian Malays adhere to Islam and that those domiciled in East Malaysia are also classified as Malaysian Malays and as part of the Bumiputera classification.

## Foodways

**Ambuyat** is identified as the official Brunei national dish and as the main dish of the Bruneian Malays. The source also names *lamban* and *punjung* as types of snack, along with other unspecified snacks.
