## Overview

The Gie Trieng people, also written as Jeh-Tariang people and Gie Trieng people, are an ethnic group in Vietnam. In Vietnamese, they are called *người Giẻ Triêng*. Most live in Kon Tum province, in Vietnam’s Central Highlands region. In 2019, their population was 63,322. They speak Jeh and Tariang, languages belonging to the Mon–Khmer language family. The sources used here provide only this concise information about their geographical distribution, population, languages, and cultural practices. Their documented funerary custom of interring the bodies of the dead by hanging coffins on trees is the only specific folk-cultural practice described in the supplied sources.
