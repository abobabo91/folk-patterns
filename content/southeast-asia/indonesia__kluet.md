## Overview

The Kluet, also called Keluwat, are a Batak sub-ethnic group in South Aceh Regency, Aceh, Indonesia. They live in North Kluet, South Kluet, Central Kluet, and East Kluet. These regions are separated by the Lawé Kluet River, which flows from Mount Leuser to the Indian Ocean. Their residential area is 30 km from Tapaktuan city and 500 km from Banda Aceh. Most of the population, including the Kluet people, practices Islam. Farming is their main source of income. The Kluet language is part of the Batak language group and has three dialects: Paya Dapur, Manggamat, and Lawe Sawah.

## Language & religion

The language spoken by the Kluet people is the Alas-Kluet language, categorized as part of the Batak languages. Kluet is divided into the Paya Dapur, Manggamat, and Lawe Sawah dialects. The majority of the population practices Islam. During the colonial period, Acehnese political and cultural influence affected the coastal area that became known as the Kluet kingdom. The Kluet adat authority structure was historically similar to that of the Acehnese.
