## Overview

The Mal are an ethnic group native to Laos and Thailand. They are one of two sub-groups of the Lua people, the other being the Phai. In Laos, the recorded population is 23,193 in Xaignabouli Province; in Thailand, it is 4,700. The Mal speak Mal, a Khmuic language within the Austroasiatic language family. They are also associated with the names Madl, Khatin, T'in, Htin, Thin, and Tin.

## Language & religion

Mal is also the name of the language spoken by the Mal. It is described as a Khmuic language, and Khmuic languages are Austroasiatic. Mal is also known as Thin or T'in. The Mal language is described as a Mon–Khmer language of Laos and Thailand and as one of several closely related languages called Thin or Prai. Tayten, with 300 speakers as of 1995, is spoken in the 2 villages of Ban Phia and Ban Tenngiou in Pakxeng District, Luang Prabang Province, Laos; the source describes it as either Thin or Tai Then.
