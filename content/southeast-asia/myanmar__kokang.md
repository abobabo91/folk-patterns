## Overview

The Kokang people are a Mandarin-speaking Han Chinese sub-ethnic group native to Kokang in Myanmar. They belong to the Sino-Tibetan family and are an officially recognized ethnic group of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar. They constitute around 0.1% of Myanmar’s population. In 1997, Kokang Chinese, together with more recently immigrated Han Chinese from Yunnan, China, were estimated to constitute 30 to 40 percent of Myanmar’s ethnic Chinese population. The available source identifies Kokang as a historical and political region.

## Language & religion

Kokang are described as Mandarin-speaking and as belonging to the Sino-Tibetan family.
