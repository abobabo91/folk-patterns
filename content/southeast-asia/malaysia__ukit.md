## Overview

The Ukit are a small minority tribe in Sarawak, Malaysia. Until recently, many lived as nomads in Borneo’s rain forests. Some settled at Rumah Ukit on the Balui River, while most are found around the Upper Rajom, Tatau, and Baleh rivers. Their population has declined through conflict, displacement, and intermarriage.

## Material culture

The profile gives little information about Ukit material culture, apart from tattoos recorded on some Ukit men.

### Clothing & dress

- **Unclothed forest life** — A description from 1880 states that the Ukit had no dwellings and were totally unclothed.
- **Tattooed upper bodies and legs** — Museum images show Ukit men with tattoos on their upper bodies and legs.

### Architecture

The sources mention Rumah Ukit.

- **Rumah Ukit** — Some Ukit settled at Rumah Ukit on the upper reaches of the Balui River.

### Jewelry & body adornment

Tattoos are the only documented form of Ukit body adornment.

- **Upper-body and leg tattoos** — Museum catalogue records show Ukit men with tattoos on their upper bodies and legs, including full-length portraits and a view of one man’s back.

## Language & religion

Ukit is a Punan language, while Punan Ukit is a dialect of Bukitan.

- **Ukit language** (*Ukit*) — Ukit is identified as a Punan language of Sarawak, Malaysia.
- **Punan Ukit dialect** (*Punan Ukit*) — Punan Ukit is described as a dialect of the related language Bukitan.

## Glossary

- *Bhuket* — Another name for the Ukit
- *Ukit* — A Punan language and the people who speak it
- *Punan Ukit* — A dialect of Bukitan
- *Bukitan* — A related language
- *Rumah Ukit* — A Ukit settlement on the Balui River
- *Balui River* — River where Rumah Ukit is located
