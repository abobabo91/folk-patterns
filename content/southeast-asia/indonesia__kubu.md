## Overview

Kubu is a Malay exonym for mobile, animist peoples living throughout the lowland forests of southeast Sumatra in Indonesia. The name refers especially to the Kubu people also called Orang Rimba, Orang Batin Sembilan, or Anak Dalam. The Orang Batin Sembilan are traditionally described as “civilized Kubu” and live in the central and eastern lowland forests of South Sumatra and Jambi, while the smaller Orang Rimba live in upstream regions of Jambi and South Sumatra. The sources give approximate populations of 35,000 in South Sumatra and 10,000 in Jambi for the Orang Batin Sembilan, and approximately 3,000 for the Orang Rimba. Kubu languages belong to the Malayo-Polynesian branch of the Austronesian language family and are related to Malay and Indonesian.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources describe the Kubu as animist and state that social hierarchies among the Orang Rimba are based partly upon knowledge of religion and culture law. The sources also state that many of these peoples have been forcibly converted to state-approved religions, primarily Islam.

## Foodways

The Orang Rimba have a diverse economy that shifts between swidden farming and a nomadic life based on foraging wild yams. This way of life is traditionally combined with hunting, trapping, fishing, and collecting forest products for trade. The Orang Batin Sembilan use swidden fields as base camps from which they exploit forest resources, particularly while collecting forest products for trade.

## Oral tradition & literature

They mention *Sokola Rimba*, a 2013 Indonesian film featuring the lifestyle of the Rimba people, and refer to Steven Sager’s work *The Sky is our Roof, the Earth our Floor: Orang Rimba Customs and Religion in the Bukit Duabelas Region of Jambi, Sumatra*.

## Language & religion

The various Kubu languages belong to the Malayo-Polynesian branch of the Austronesian language family. They are isolects of the Malay language spoken in the upstream regions of South Sumatra and Jambi, and they are related to Indonesian, which is based upon a variant of Malay. Kubu is a Malayic language spoken in the southern swamps of Sumatra in Indonesia by the Kubu people, many of whom are nomadic, and it has a degree of dialectal diversity. In Bukit Duabelas in Jambi, the Rimba language is described as very glottal and initially difficult to understand. The Orang Rimba are described as animist, while many members of these peoples have been forcibly converted to state-approved religions, primarily Islam.
