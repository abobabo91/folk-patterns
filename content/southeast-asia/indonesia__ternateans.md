## Overview

The Ternate people are located on Ternate Island, where their population is 50,000 people. Ternate Island is included in the province of North Maluku, whose capital city is Ternate. In addition to Ternate Island, Ternate people live in the Bacan Islands and Obi Islands, which are part of the South Halmahera Regency, as well as in other areas inside and outside North Maluku Province. Most Ternate people are Muslims, with a small Protestant minority. They speak the Ternate language, a non-Austronesian language belonging to the North Halmahera branch of the West Papuan languages.

## Language & religion

The Ternate language is a non-Austronesian language belonging to the North Halmahera branch of the West Papuan languages. Most Ternate people are Muslims, and there is also a small Protestant minority.
