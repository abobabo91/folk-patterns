## Overview

The Tuscarora Nation is a federally recognized Tuscarora tribe based in Lewiston, New York. The Tuscarora migrated from the New York area to the Carolinas in ancient times, then moved north after the Tuscarora War. Most settled in Haudenosaunee territory beginning in 1722 and declared the nation fully relocated by 1765. The profile gives little information about Tuscarora folk culture.

## Music & performance

The sources do not document a musical tradition, but one wartime account mentions horns and war whoops.

- **Horns and war whoops** — During the attack on Lewiston in 1813, one Tuscarora party blew horns while another attacked with war whoops to make the group seem larger.
