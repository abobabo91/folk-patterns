## Overview

The Tawakoni, also known as Tahuacano and Tehuacana, are a Southern Plains Native American tribe closely related to the Wichitas. At the beginning of the 18th century, they lived in villages in what is now Oklahoma and Texas. In 1719, Jean Baptiste Bénard de La Harpe encountered a Tawakoni village in present-day Muskogee County, Oklahoma. The Tawakoni historically spoke a Wichita language of the Caddoan language family and currently are enrolled in the Wichita and Affiliated Tribes, a federally recognized tribe. Their documented history includes maize and tobacco cultivation, diplomacy, treaties, reservation life, forced relocation, and incorporation into wider Wichita political organization.
