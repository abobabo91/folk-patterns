## Overview

The Tuscarora Nation (*Skarù·ręʔ Kayedá·kreh*) is a federally recognized tribe of Tuscarora people. Its headquarters is on the Tuscarora Reservation in Lewiston, New York, where its elected chief is Tom Jonathan. The Tuscarora migrated in ancient times from the New York area to the Carolinas. After their defeat in the Tuscarora War at the beginning of the 18th century, most migrated north to Haudenosaunee territory beginning in 1722. They first settled in the territory of the Oneida people in central New York and declared their nation fully relocated by 1765.

## Material culture

### Textile & pattern traditions

**Beaded floral motif decoration** — The museum catalogue records baby carriers decorated with a beaded floral motif. The records do not identify a vernacular name, technique, or comparison with neighboring cultures.

**Cloth dresses** — The catalogue records Tuscarora women and girls wearing cloth dresses.

**Baby carriers** — The catalogue records baby carriers in which babies were wrapped in bundles. Several carriers are described as having beaded floral motif decoration.

### Clothing & dress

The museum catalogue records a Tuscarora woman wearing a cloth dress and holding a baby in a baby carrier. Another catalogue record describes a Tuscarora (?) woman wearing a cloth head covering and cloth dress, while a girl beside her wears a hat, necklace, cloth dress, and boots. The babies are described as wrapped in bundles attached to baby carriers.

### Architecture

The sources state that the Tuscarora Nation’s headquarters is located on the Tuscarora Reservation in Lewiston, New York.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The museum catalogue records baby carriers, including carriers with beaded floral motif decoration.

### Jewelry & body adornment

One museum catalogue record states that a girl wore a necklace, hat, cloth dress, and boots.

## Music & performance

The Wikipedia account states that during the attack on Lewiston, New York, on December 19, 1813, one Tuscarora party blew horns along the escarpment and another party attacked downhill with war whoops to give an exaggerated impression of the Tuscarora numbers.

## Festivals & rituals

The Wikipedia account describes the Tuscarora War, the migration of most Tuscarora north to Haudenosaunee territory beginning in 1722, and the declaration in 1765 that the nation was fully relocated.

## Oral tradition & literature

The Wikipedia article identifies David Cusick as an artist and author and John Napoleon Brinton Hewitt as a linguist and ethnographer.

## Language & religion

The Wikipedia article gives *Skarù·ręʔ Kayedá·kreh* as the Tuscarora name for the Tuscarora Nation.
