## Overview

The Chelan are an Interior Salish people who speak the Wenatchi dialect, although they are separate from that tribe. Their name derives from the traditional Wenatchi name Tsi-Laan, meaning “deep water.” Historically, they lived at the outlet of Lake Chelan in Washington, where they spent the winter months. Lake Chelan flows into the Columbia River through the Chelan River, and the Chelan used its waters and connected routes for fishing, transportation, trade, and travel. They also had permanent villages in the lower Chelan valley and moved seasonally through the mountains, collecting plants, fishing small streams, and hunting game. Today, the Chelan are members of the Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation.

## Material culture

### Architecture

The Chelan had several permanent villages in the lower Chelan valley. One village at Willow Point, near Manson, had up to 500 occupants, while another on Wapato Point was home to about 100 people.

### Ceramics, metalwork & everyday objects

The sources state that the Chelan used canoes for travel and trade, and that seashells acquired through exchange were used for future trade or ornamentation.

### Jewelry & body adornment

Seashells obtained through trade are identified as objects used for ornamentation.

## Festivals & rituals

The sources describe seasonal movement through the landscape: the Chelan spent winter months at the outlet of Lake Chelan, fished during salmon runs, and traveled in and out of the mountains with the seasons.

## Foodways

The sources used document fishing, hunting, plant collecting, and trade. During salmon runs, the Chelan fished at the outlet where Lake Chelan meets the river and also traveled to the Wenatshapam Fishery on the Columbia River to fish and trade with other tribes. They exchanged mountain goat wool for dried clams and salmon, as well as for seashells.

## Oral tradition & literature

The source list identifies an external collection titled “Chelan legends - ‘Creation of the first Indians,’” but provides no further description of its contents.

## Language & religion

The Chelan speak the Wenatchi dialect and are described as an Interior Salish people. Their native language is identified as a Salishan language made up of several different dialects among the tribes, while English is also spoken.
