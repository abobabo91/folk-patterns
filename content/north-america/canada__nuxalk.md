## Overview

The Nuxalk people, also referred to as the Bella Coola, Bellacoola, or Bilchula, are a First Nation of the Pacific Northwest Coast centred in and around Bella Coola, British Columbia, within their wider traditional territory, Kulhulmcilh. Their on-reserve tribal government is the Nuxalk Nation. The Nuxalk population is estimated at approximately 1,400 by current Indian and Northern Affairs Canada figures, with nearly 900 living on the Nuxalk reserve in Bella Coola; the traditional Nuxalk government gives a figure closer to 3,000, including people of Nuxalk ancestry not registered with the Nuxalk Nation or registered to another band government. Nuxalk is an endangered Salishan language. Family ancestry, villages of descent, crests, songs, dances, mythology, and continuing systems of governance remain important aspects of Nuxalk culture.

## Music & performance

Nuxalk songs and dances recount family history and myth in *smayusta*. Knowledge of family ancestry includes villages of descent, family crests, songs, and dances, and these forms remain connected to Nuxalk historical and mythological knowledge.

The documentary films of Banchi Hanuse profile Nuxalk culture and society, including *Nuxalk Radio* (2020) and *Ceremony* (2026). The Nuxalk language source also states that CKNN-FM Nuxalk Radio works to promote the language.

## Festivals & rituals

They state that Nuxalk religion includes belief in Alhkwʼntam, the creator god; Qamaits, the war goddess; and Qʼumakwa, the sea god, among others.

Nuxalk religious tradition also names Masmasalanixw, the Four Supernatural Carpenters who carved the first human beings; Snx, the sun god; Talyu, Qʼumakwa’s wife; S7ayulh, the thunderbird; Sisyulh, a two-headed sea serpent; Tcncniyaaq, the cosmic tree identified with the Milky Way; and Qwaxw, Raven, the trickster. The four catastrophes are the falling of the sky, the burning of the world, the flood, and the famine.

## Foodways

The Nuxalk language source includes words translated as “animal fat” and “fat, grease,”.

## Oral tradition & literature

Nuxalk oral histories and academic research are cited in connection with population history. Family ancestry remains strong, including knowledge of villages of descent, family crests, and songs and dances recounting history and myth in *smayusta*. The Nuxalk language source provides narrative examples involving Raven and Mink, canoe travel, halibut fishing, Cormorant, a young boy, and a man.

Nuxalk religious narratives identify Qwaxw, Raven, as a trickster and describe Masmasalanixw, the Four Supernatural Carpenters, carving the first human beings. They also include the four catastrophes: the falling of the sky, the burning of the world, the flood, and the famine.

## Language & religion

Nuxalk, also known as Bella Coola, is a Salishan language spoken by the Nuxalk people. It forms its own subgroup of the Salish language family and shares phonological and morphological features with Coast Salish. It also borrows words from contiguous North Wakashan languages, especially Heiltsuk, and from neighbouring Athabaskan and Tsimshianic languages. Nuxalk is now spoken only in Bella Coola, British Columbia; it was once spoken in more than 100 settlements, with varying dialects. The language is endangered, although it is taught in the provincial school system and at Acwsalcta, “a place of learning.”

The native name Nuxalk is preferred by the Nuxalk Nation’s government, while Bella Coola derives from the Heiltsuk name bḷ́xʷlá, meaning “stranger.” The language has a practical orthography used in Nuxalk documents, names, and language learning. Nuxalk is described as a polysynthetic language with polypersonal agreement and as a language with long strings of consonants, including words and phrases without intervening vowels. Personal pronouns are reportedly nonexistent, with the idea expressed through verbs translating as “to be me” and similar forms.

Nuxalk religion includes belief in a creator god, a war goddess, a sea god, the sun god, the thunderbird, a two-headed sea serpent, a cosmic tree, and Raven as trickster.
