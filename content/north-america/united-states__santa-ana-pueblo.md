## Overview

Santa Ana Pueblo, also called *Tamaya* in Eastern Keres, is a settlement in Sandoval County, New Mexico, in the Rio Grande valley and part of the Albuquerque metropolitan area. Its people speak an eastern dialect of the Keresan languages. The United States Census Bureau defined the community as a census-designated place; in the 2000 census, it had 479 residents. The Pueblo administers 73,000 acres of reservation land. The available source presents Santa Ana Pueblo as a community whose contemporary economy includes work outside Pueblo lands, arts and crafts, gardening, cooking enterprises, tourism, gaming, and golf.

## Festivals & rituals

Santa Ana Pueblo celebrates an annual feast day for its patron saint, St. Anne, on July 26.

## Foodways

The sources state that Santa Ana Pueblo operates cooking enterprises.
