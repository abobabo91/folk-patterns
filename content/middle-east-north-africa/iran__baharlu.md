## Overview

The Baharlu (Azerbaijani: بهارلی; Persian: بهارلو), also spelled Bahārlou, Baharloo, and Baharlooe, are a Turkic ethnic group living in Hamadan, Fars, Kerman, Azerbaijan, and Khorasan in Iran. Fars contains the bulk of Baharlu tribe members and is identified as the provenance of the tribe. The Baharlu were historically part of the Khamseh confederation. Their history is connected with the Qara Qoyunlu, Aq Qoyunlu, Shamlu, Qizilbash, Baranlı or Barani, and Yiwa tribes.

## Language & religion

The sources identify the Baharlu as a Turkic ethnic group and give Azerbaijani and Persian forms of their name.
