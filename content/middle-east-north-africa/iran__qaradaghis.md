## Overview

Qaradaghis, also known as Karadaghis or *Qaradağlılar*, are a Turkic sub-ethnic group of Azerbaijanis. They mainly live in Qaradagh, in the Southern Aras river area of Eastern Azerbaijan, Iran.

## Language & religion

The Qaradaghis are predominantly Shi'a Muslim and speak the Qaradaghi dialect of the Azerbaijani language.
