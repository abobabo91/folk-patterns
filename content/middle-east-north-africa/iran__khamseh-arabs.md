## Overview

The Khamseh Arabs are an Arab nomadic and pastoral tribe of Fars province in Iran and form part of the Khamseh confederation. Their population numbers about 200,000. They speak a dialect of Central Asian Arabic that is closely related to Uzbeki Arabic and difficult for other Arabic speakers to understand. The two Arab tribes in Fars province are Sheybani and Jabbareh. The Sheybani migrated to Iran before the Jabbareh, first settling in Khorasan before later moving to Fars district. The Jabbareh of Fars say they descend from Jabir ibn Abd Allah, a companion of Muhammad. A group of Sheybani live in Sheyban city of Bavi County in Khuzestan.

## Oral tradition & literature

The Jabbareh account identifies Jabir ibn Abd Allah as a companion of Muhammad and says that their ancestor Sheikh Jinaah migrated to Fars, married a Sheybani girl, and named his descendants “Jabbareh.”

## Language & religion

Khamseh Arabs speak a dialect of Central Asian Arabic. It is closely related to Uzbeki Arabic, is difficult for other Arabic speakers to understand, and has no relations to Gulf Arabic. Khamseh Arabs are mostly Shia Muslims.
