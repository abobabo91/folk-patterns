## Overview

Khorasani Turks are a Turkic ethnic group inhabiting parts of North Khorasan, Razavi Khorasan and Golestan provinces of Iran, as well as neighboring regions of Turkmenistan extending beyond the Amu Darya River. They speak Khorasani Turkic. Some also speak Kurdish because of intermarriages with Khorasani Kurds, while Persian functions as the lingua franca of Iran. They distinguish Khorasani Turks from other Turkic groups that arrived in Khorasan more recently, especially Iranian Azerbaijanis, who had a presence in the area, particularly in Mashhad, from about the early 20th century.
