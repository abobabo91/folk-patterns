# World peoples with museum evidence

Generated by `python scripts/world_peoples.py report` — do not edit by hand.

A people is **listed** when its objects fill 2+ of the 12 categories with 3+ objects each (column **cat. 3+**). It is counted at all when the museums with a people field hold 6+ image objects under its name: the British Museum "Ethnic group" and the Met and Cleveland culture fields (the Met: public domain, 1700 or later). **Met+Cle** is that count. The Met's European entries (French, German) come from its costume and arms departments, not folk collections. The V&A names places, not peoples, so it cannot be counted per people. Smithsonian anthropology has no open images. **Breadth** is how many of the 12 object categories (photo and unclassified excluded) have 5+ objects in a sample of up to 500 BM objects plus every Met/Cleveland match. Categories come from each object's BM name via `normalize_kinds.py` (Haiku). **BM** is capped at 500 by the sample. **Eur.** counts records at ethnographic providers in Europeana that mention the name. It is a text match, so it is only a hint. Peoples only Europeana finds are listed separately, because most of those are word collisions ("Iron" for Ossetians, "Bali", "Dan").
